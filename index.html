An archive begun in 2026 · Caraga, Philippines

Two islands.
One family's record.

Dinagat Heritage traces a family carried between two shores — my mother's people from the Dinagat Islands, my father's from Tagbilaran, Bohol — and sets it against the wider record of the province itself: its Mamanwa roots, its churches, its civil registries, and the long paper trail that outlives everyone who signed it.

Est.
1855

Dinagat is founded as a municipality

The oldest settled community on the island, formed under Spanish colonial order — long after the Mamanwa had already made it home.

Rec.
1907 — Cortes, Bohol

The earliest record found, so far

A paternal-line ancestor, born in Cortes and gone by 1955 — the furthest back the paper trail currently reaches. Every record before this one is still missing, not disproven.

Est.
2006

Dinagat becomes its own province

Republic Act No. 9355 separated Dinagat from Surigao del Norte — a province young enough that the people who remember its founding are still telling the story themselves.
