This archive is only as good as what people are willing to hand over — a name, a photo, a story someone told you once that you never wrote down. If any of that sounds familiar, this is where it goes.

Share a story or photo

Use the form below for anything with real content — a memory, a name and how they connect to the family, a photo description, a correction to something already on the site. This gets logged properly so it doesn't just disappear into an inbox.

Or just email it over

If a form feels like overkill for a two-line memory, that's fine too — send it straight to the source.

Not a fan of typing? Just draw it

Sometimes a quick sketch beats a form. Write your name in the middle of a piece of paper, draw a line up to your parents, lines down to your kids, lines sideways to your siblings — that's it. Doesn't need to be neat. Take a photo of it and send it over the same way as anything else — email, Facebook, or the form above. We'll take it from there.
